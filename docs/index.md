# Leitwerk

<p class="lw-hero__lead">AI agents do the delivery work. Your team decides at the steps that matter.</p>

<p class="lw-hero__sub">Leitwerk runs each piece of work as a process: it notices the work, does it with an AI agent in a sandbox, and brings in people at the decisions the process defines. Pick a process to watch it run.</p>
