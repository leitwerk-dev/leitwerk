# Solution wikis

A solution wiki keeps reusable guidance from processes working on the same topic, such as an epic. Each entry explains where a solution applies and points to the evidence behind it.

## Find guidance for a topic

1. Open **Solution wikis** in the sidebar and choose a topic. If a process is linked to a topic, you can also open **Solution wiki** from its header.
2. Use **Find an entry** to search by title, applicability, or text. Use **Evidence status** to narrow the list.
3. Read **Applies when** to check whether the guidance fits your work. Check the cited files and commits under **Evidence** before using it. Follow **Contributing process** if you need the original context.

## Edit entries and inspect history

1. Click **Edit entry**. Update **Applies when** and **Entry text (Markdown)** so the next reader knows where the solution works and what limits it has.
2. Keep at least one **Evidence** item with a repository, path, commit SHA, and observation. For an entry marked **Needs revalidation**, check what changed in the cited evidence or related entries before updating the evidence status.
3. Click **Save changes**. Use **Show revision history** to see earlier versions.

If someone changes the entry while you're editing, Leitwerk keeps your draft. Copy any edits you want to keep before clicking **Reload latest entry**, then reapply them to the latest version.

## Delete entries or wiki groups

To remove one entry, open it, choose **Delete entry…**, and confirm. To remove a topic and all of its entries, choose **Delete group…** from **Solution wikis** or **Delete wiki group…** in the topic header, then confirm. Running processes may still have guidance they read before deletion.
